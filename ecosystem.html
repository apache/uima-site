Get your project listed

If you maintain a project that builds on Apache UIMA, or know of one missing here, write to the dev mailing list or open a pull request against _data/ecosystem.yml. The same applies if an entry is out of date.

The projects listed here are maintained by their own communities. They are not part of Apache UIMA, and the Apache UIMA project does not endorse or support them.
